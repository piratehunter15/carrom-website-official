Tournament Federation Rules

Official Match Directives

01

Surface Conditioning & Powder

scatter_plot

Boards are lightly conditioned with approved non-toxic powder exclusively by official table marshals prior to each match. Competitors may not apply their own talc, chalk, or foreign agents to the board perimeter or pocket netting.

02

Striking Mechanics & Base Line

touch_app

The striker must touch both baselines and must not cut the baseline circles. The strike must be cleanly flicked with a single finger or thumb. Pushing, guiding, double-striking, or sliding the hand forward beyond the base line results in an immediate foul penalty.

03

The Queen (Red) Cover Protocol

stars

Pocketing the Queen requires immediate validation (“cover”). The competitor must sink an additional carrom man of their designated color on the same turn or the immediate subsequent shot. Failure to cover returns the Queen to the center circle.

04

Striker Pockets, Dues & Fouls

gavel

Pocketing the striker is a foul incurring a 1-piece penalty (“due”). The referee places the penalty piece inside the outer center circle. If no pieces have been pocketed by the offender, the due is banked until the next legal pocket.
