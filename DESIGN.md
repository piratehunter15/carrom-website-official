## Brand & Style
The design system channels the heritage, precision, and grandeur of world-class carrom tournaments and aristocratic championship saloons. It communicates absolute mastery, high-stakes tournament prestige, and classical craftsmanship. 

The aesthetic blends **Tactile Classical Elegance** with **Refined Editorial Minimalism**:
- **Tone:** Prestigious, deliberate, commanding, and timelessly luxurious.
- **Visual Tenets:** Deep polished rosewood and rich mahogany framing elements, luminous ivory playing surfaces, laser-fine brass inlays, and deep ceremonial crimson accents symbolizing the coveted Carrom Queen.
- **Emotional Intent:** Evoke the quiet tension and regal focus of a high-stakes Grandmaster final match held in a heritage wood-paneled pavilion.

## Colors
The palette mirrors the tactile materiality of a bespoke master tournament board:

- **Primary (`#2A1810` - Deep Obsidian Rosewood):** Serves as structural anchor, deep framing borders, high-contrast typography, and primary interactive containers.
- **Secondary (`#C9971F` - Burnished Championship Gold):** Represents championship glory, brass corner hardware, rank distinctions, active indicators, and focal points.
- **Tertiary (`#8B1E2D` - Antique Queen Ruby):** Reserved for high-value alerts, champion designations, score emphasis, and prestige tournament highlights.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FCFAF6` - Warm Ivory Parchment):** The low-strain, hyper-polished court field surface. Layered alongside a secondary neutral base (`#F5EFE6` - Polished Boxwood) for inner container delineation.
- **Supporting Accents:** Burnished brass borders (`#D4AF37`) and deep archival charcoal (`#1A1412`) for ultra-high legibility on small-scale statistics.

## Typography
The typographical hierarchy balances classical heritage with tournament scorekeeping legibility:

- **Headlines (Domine):** Sturdy, authoritative transitional serif echoing championship certificates, historic arena inscriptions, and master-level pedigree.
- **Body (Manrope):** Geometric precision engineered for high readability, neutral tone, and clear editorial flow across match summaries, match history, and rulesets.
- **Labels & Metrics (Space Grotesk):** Precise, technical, and slightly mechanical sans-serif optimized for strike power meters, shot clocks, Elo ratings, and board coordinate labeling.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout reflects the architectural discipline of a carrom board: symmetrical, bounded, and deliberate.

- **Structure:** 12-column responsive fluid grid bounded within a maximum viewport width of 1440px to emulate an enclosed playing court.
- **Mobile Adaptations:** Folds into a 4-column structure with tightened margins (`1.25rem`) and unified card spacing (`0.75rem`), prioritizing player telemetry, board views, and live bracket columns.
- **Rhythm:** Spacing follows strict multiples of `4px` / `8px`, prioritizing expansive negative space around hero match displays and compressed, razor-sharp padding within tournament data grids.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is articulated through tactile layering that mimics hand-lacquered wood, brass bevels, and recessed playing beds:

- **Level 0 (Arena Surface):** `#FCFAF6` matte finish parchment-grade canvas.
- **Level 1 (Court Inset Cards):** `#FFFFFF` surfaces bounded by a 1px border of `rgba(201, 151, 31, 0.2)` (Burnished Brass Outline) and an ambient shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(42, 24, 16, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Play / Raised Modals):** Lacquered depth with a multi-layered cast: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(42, 24, 16, 0.12), 0 2px 6px 0 rgba(42, 24, 16, 0.04)`.
- **Framing & Accents:** Hero containers and primary court views utilize a dual-border treatment: an outer 2px `#2A1810` rosewood boundary accompanied by an inner 1px `#C9971F` gold foil rim.

## Shapes
A disciplined, conservative radius profile (`soft` - 4px default) captures the tailored joinery of handcrafted tournament boards.

- **Micro-radii (4px):** Applied to buttons, input fields, badges, and score cells.
- **Medium-radii (8px):** Structural cards, match summary panes, and tournament bracket blocks.
- **Signature Circular Details:** True circular elements (`rounded-full`) are reserved exclusively for player avatars, striker puck controls, coin counters, and match position indicators.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Grandmaster Strike):** Solid `#2A1810` background, `#FCFAF6` text, 1px inset border `#C9971F`. Hover transitions to subtle gold wash (`#3A241A`) with elevated brass border glow.
- **Secondary (Brass Court):** Transparent background, 1px border of `#C9971F`, `#2A1810` typography. Hover creates `#F5EFE6` fill.
- **Prestige Action (Queen Ruby):** `#8B1E2D` solid background, `#FCFAF6` text, used exclusively for high-stakes calls, match confirmations, or sudden death plays.

### Cards & Tournament Containers
- Constructed on ivory canvas (`#FFFFFF` or `#F7F3EB`), framed with subtle 1px metallic brass edges (`rgba(201, 151, 31, 0.25)`).
- Headers display `Domine` with a trailing gold divider rule (`1px` solid `#E8DFD1`).

### Chips & Badges
- **Elo & Rank Chips:** Pill-shaped, subtle ivory tint with `#2A1810` borders and `Space Grotesk` uppercase typography.
- **Queen Indicator:** Velvet ruby badge (`#8B1E2D`) with gold foiled text (`#F5EFE6`).

### Inputs & Selectors
- Flat ivory fields (`#FFFFFF`) with 1px structural wood borders (`#D6CBBF`). Focus state transitions border to `#C9971F` with a soft 2px ambient brass glow.

### Brackets & Match Nodes
- High-contrast nodes interconnected with 2px burnished brass lines (`#C9971F`). Winner routes are emphasized with double-line gold detailing and subtle ruby corner markers.
