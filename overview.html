Structure & Equipment

Regulations

Standard Carrom Rules

Matches follow standard international/national carrom guidelines:

  • Board must be properly powdered before play commences.
  • Strikers must be flicked, not pushed; thumb or finger strikes only from within the baseline.
  • Queen must be covered by pocketing a subsequent piece of the same player's color in the same turn.
  • Board dues, white/black pieces scoring, and foul penalties apply as per standard federation code.
