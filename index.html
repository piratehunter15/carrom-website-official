Standard Equipment & Play Protocol

  • check_circle10–15 Competition Boards: English Birchwood tournament frames and smooth carrom boards provided directly by the school.
  • check_circleEquipment Provided: All tournament strikers and carrom coins are standardized and supplied by the school for fair play.
  • check_circleQueen Point Value: The Queen carries 3 points. A player sinking the Queen must sink a cover coin in the same or immediately following turn.
  • check_circleFoul Line Enforcements: Touching baseline demarcations with anything other than striking fingers results in a 1-coin penalty return.
